Korea: 5 places you must visit

From royal palaces to ocean sunrises — my favorite spots in Korea.

1

Gyeongbokgung Palace

Seoul

The largest royal palace of the Joseon Dynasty, built in 1395. Walk through the big gates and see the beautiful wooden buildings with the mountains behind them. If you wear a hanbok, you can enter for free!

Tip: Watch the Royal Guard Changing Ceremony at the main gate.

2

N Seoul Tower

Namsan, Seoul

A tower on top of Namsan Mountain with a view of the whole city. You can hike up or take the cable car. Couples hang "love locks" on the fences, and the night view of Seoul is amazing.

Tip: Go just before sunset to see the city in daylight and at night.

3

Haeundae Beach

Busan

The most famous beach in Korea, with soft sand and tall buildings along the coast. In summer it is full of people swimming, and in the evening you can walk along the beach and eat fresh seafood nearby.

Tip: Ride the Haeundae Blueline Park sky capsule along the coast.

4

Bulguksa Temple

Gyeongju

A Buddhist temple from the Silla Kingdom and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Gyeongju was the capital of Silla for almost 1,000 years, so the whole city feels like an outdoor museum.

Tip: Visit in spring when the cherry blossoms are blooming.

5

Seongsan Ilchulbong

Jeju Island

A huge volcanic crater that rises out of the ocean. It takes about 30 minutes to climb to the top, and the view of the sea and the green crater is unforgettable. It is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Tip: Climb early in the morning to watch the sunrise.
